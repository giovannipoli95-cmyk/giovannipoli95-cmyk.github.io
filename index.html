About the Conference

AISTayes 2027 will be hosted by the University of Tolence. The conference will bring together researchers working on Bayesian statistics, computation and applications, providing a forum for the exchange of methodological developments, scientific ideas and applied perspectives.

Further information about dates, registration, submissions and the scientific programme will be published on this website.
