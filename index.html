About the Conference

ABC-Diees 2027 will be hosted by the University of Aclerebe. The conference will bring together researchers working on Bayesian statistics, computation and applications, providing a forum for the exchange of methodological developments, scientific ideas and applied perspectives.

Further information about registration, submissions and the scientific programme will be published on this website.
